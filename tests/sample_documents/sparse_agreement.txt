SHORT-FORM CONSULTING LETTER AGREEMENT

This letter agreement is made as of June 2, 2026 between Kaveri Design Studio
("Consultant") and Bramble Foods Co. ("Company").

1. SCOPE

Consultant will provide brand identity design services for the Company's
packaging refresh. Consultant will deliver three concept directions and one
final artwork package.

2. FEES

The Company will pay Consultant a flat fee of twelve thousand United States
dollars ($12,000) for the services described above. The fee is payable in two
equal instalments: fifty percent on signature and fifty percent on delivery of
the final artwork package.

3. EXPENSES

The Company will reimburse Consultant for pre-approved travel expenses
supported by receipts.

4. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR

Consultant is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner, or agent
of the Company. Consultant is responsible for its own taxes.

5. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This letter is the entire agreement between the parties on this subject.

Signed for and on behalf of Kaveri Design Studio and Bramble Foods Co.
