MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made and entered into as of
January 9, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Helios Cloud Systems, Inc.
("Provider") and Vantage Retail Group LLC ("Client").

1. SERVICES AND OBLIGATIONS

1.1 Provider shall perform the consulting and managed hosting services described
in each Statement of Work executed by the Parties (the "Services").

1.2 Provider shall deliver each milestone deliverable no later than the
applicable milestone date set out in the relevant Statement of Work and shall
provide written progress reports to Client on the first business day of each
calendar month.

1.3 Client shall designate a single project manager with authority to approve
deliverables, and shall provide Provider with timely access to systems,
personnel, and information reasonably required to perform the Services.

1.4 Client shall review and either accept or reject each deliverable in writing
within ten (10) business days of receipt. Failure to respond within that period
shall be deemed acceptance of the deliverable.

2. FEES AND PAYMENT

2.1 Client shall pay Provider the fees set out in each Statement of Work. Unless
a Statement of Work provides otherwise, Provider shall invoice monthly in
arrears and Client shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of
the invoice date.

2.2 Any amount not paid when due shall accrue interest at the lesser of one and
one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by applicable
law, calculated from the due date until payment is received.

2.3 If any undisputed invoice remains unpaid for more than sixty (60) days after
its due date, Provider may suspend the Services upon ten (10) days' written
notice without liability, and Client shall reimburse Provider for reasonable
costs of collection, including attorneys' fees.

2.4 All fees are exclusive of applicable taxes, which shall be borne by Client.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION

3.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial
term of three (3) years (the "Initial Term"), renewing automatically for
successive one (1) year periods unless either Party gives written notice of
non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

3.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60)
days' prior written notice.

3.3 Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately for cause if the other
Party commits a material breach and fails to cure that breach within thirty (30)
days after receiving written notice describing the breach in reasonable detail.

3.4 Provider may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if
Client becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of
creditors, or has a receiver appointed over a substantial part of its assets.

3.5 Upon termination, Client shall pay Provider for all Services performed and
expenses incurred through the effective date of termination, and Provider shall
deliver to Client all work product paid for as of that date.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 Each Party shall hold the other Party's confidential information in strict
confidence and shall not use it except as necessary to perform or receive the
Services. These obligations survive termination for five (5) years.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 Provider retains all right, title, and interest in its pre-existing
materials, tools, and know-how. Client owns the deliverables specifically
created for Client under a Statement of Work upon full payment of the associated
fees.

5.2 Provider grants Client a non-exclusive, worldwide, perpetual license to use
any Provider background materials embedded in the deliverables.

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

6.1 Except for a Party's indemnification obligations, breach of confidentiality,
or gross negligence or willful misconduct, neither Party shall be liable for any
indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages, or for lost
profits or revenue, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

6.2 Except as set out in Section 6.1, each Party's total aggregate liability
arising out of or related to this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid
or payable by Client under the applicable Statement of Work during the twelve
(12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

7. INDEMNIFICATION

7.1 Provider shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Client from and against
any third-party claim alleging that the deliverables infringe a United States
patent, copyright, or trademark, provided Client promptly notifies Provider and
grants Provider sole control of the defense.

7.2 Client shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Provider from and against
any third-party claim arising from Client's data, Client's instructions, or
Client's use of the deliverables in violation of applicable law.

8. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

8.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California, excluding
its conflict of laws rules.

8.2 The Parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute through
negotiation. If the dispute is not resolved within thirty (30) days, either Party
may bring an action in the state or federal courts located in San Francisco
County, California, and each Party consents to venue and personal jurisdiction
there.

9. GENERAL

9.1 Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior
written consent, except to a successor in connection with a merger or sale of
substantially all assets.

9.2 This Agreement, together with all Statements of Work, is the entire agreement
between the Parties and supersedes all prior proposals and understandings.
